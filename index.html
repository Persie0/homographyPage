QUALITY CHECK

What should a correct result look like?

The road's painted parallel lines should look approximately parallel and straight, the 5 m grid should follow the actual surface, and cars should occupy realistic ground distances. A bird's-eye image can look convincing even if the metric scale is wrong: verify using a second known length or real vehicle motion.
